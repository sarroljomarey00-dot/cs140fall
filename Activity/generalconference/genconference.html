Top Three Talk from General Conference

  • Elder Jeremiah Morgan
  • Elder David A. Bednar
  • President Dallin H. Oaks

"The Infinite Power of God in Our Covenants"

Covenants will help us fulfill the mission of God upon us. Being bound, connected, or covenanted to Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ means that we are agreeing to the will of God and trusting Them that we will receive the promised blessings They have told us about as we follow Them.


"Sealed by the Holy Spirit of Promise"

All the promises that God gives to His prophet from Adam to the current prophet is being fulfilled. His promises are not just for a certain time frame, but it is for eternaly. We can obtain all the promises that He gave to us when we follow His will and use our moral agency to always choose Him. No one can take that from us. How we make and keep covenants will determine how God can guide and bless out lives here one by one.


"The Gospel of Jesus Christ Is for All"

President Oaks talks taught me that I need to refocus on the Savior Jesus Christ. He gave us the way, the truth, and the light on how we can get back home. The scriptures tell us who He is and what His capability is. This is our mission as a member of the Church of Jesus Christ, to help all men and women to know and come unto Christ. He can help us, He can guide us, He can show us how to get to our Father in Heaven.
