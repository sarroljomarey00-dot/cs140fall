Science Building

The Science Building at Brigham Young University–Hawaii is a place where students learn about math and science. It has modern classrooms, laboratories, and spaces for research and group activities. Students can practice what they learn through experiments and hands-on activities. The building helps students develop their knowledge and skills for their future careers.
