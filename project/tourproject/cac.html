George Q. Cannon Activities Center

The Cannon Activities Center (CAC) is a large sports and event building at Brigham Young University–Hawaii. It is used for sports games, concerts, performances, and other university events. Students and visitors gather here to enjoy activities and support their school. It is an important place for sports and entertainment on campus.
