Brigham Young University-Hawaii Campus

Brigham Young University–Hawaii (BYU–Hawaii) is a beautiful campus located on the North Shore of Oahu, Hawaii. This website helps people explore the campus by introducing five places: the Cannon Activities Center, Banyan Dining Hall, Heber J. Grant Building, Science Building, and Stake Center. Each location has its own purpose and is an important part of student life at BYU–Hawaii. Through this website, visitors and new students can learn more about these places and get a better idea of what the campus looks like.
