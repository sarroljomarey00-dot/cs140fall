Stake Center

The Stake Center at Brigham Young University–Hawaii is a religious building used for worship services, church meetings, and spiritual activities. It has chapels and rooms for classes and meetings. Students and members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints gather here to learn about the gospel, strengthen their faith, and build friendships. It is an important place for spiritual growth and community on campus.
