Banyan Dining Hall

The Banyan Dining Hall is the main cafeteria at Brigham Young University–Hawaii. It offers a variety of foods from different cultures, including Asian, Filipino, and American dishes. Students can enjoy their meals, meet friends, and spend time together in a comfortable place. It helps bring students from different countries and cultures together through food.
