Heber J. Grant Building

The Heber J. Grant Building at Brigham Young University–Hawaii is a multipurpose building used for classes, faculty offices, and student activities. It houses departments such as business management, hospitality and tourism management, and accounting. Students can attend classes, meet with teachers, and participate in university activities here. The building is named after Heber J. Grant, the seventh president of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.
