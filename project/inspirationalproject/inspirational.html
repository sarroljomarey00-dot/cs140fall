President Dieter F. Uchtdorf

President Dieter F. Uchtdorf was born on November 6, 1940, in Ostrava, Czechoslovakia. He joined the German Air Force for six years and become an aviator, executive, Chief Pilot, head of pilot school, head cockpit and cabin crews, and senior vice president of flight operations for Lufthansa German Airlines. He was sustained as an Apostle on October 2, 2004, and served as Second Counselor in the First Presidency with President Monson and Elder Eyring.

"...compared to God, man is nothing; yet we are everything to God."

I love how he always relates all his talk about his experience in his aviation career. It helps illustrate his talks about the gospel of Jesus Christ. I like how he makes emphasized our identity as a child of God, who is entitled to all the blessings God promised to the prophets of old. In addition, the way he tells us how important we are to the Heavenly Father will surely help us to have confident in Christ and our Father in heaven that They are always by our side.
